Step 3 — It gets smarter

The more you use it,
the more it knows you

Most tools treat every stuck moment the same. We remember. After enough sessions, it starts recognizing the patterns in how your brain works — and the help gets sharper because of it.

①
Your patterns start to emerge
"You hit fear of failure on 80% of work tasks — even small ones."
Every session adds a data point. Over time the app recognizes which patterns show up most for you, on which kinds of tasks, and at which times of day — without you having to track anything.
②
Interventions get personalized
"The 'one sentence' trick works for you. The timer doesn't."
When the app knows which interventions have worked for you before — and which haven't — it stops suggesting the ones that don't land. The recommendations become yours specifically, not generic ADHD advice.
③
Dopamine rewards stay fresh
"You've used the coffee shop reward 6 times — here's something new."
ADHD brains habituate fast. The app tracks which rewards you've been leaning on and rotates in alternatives before they stop working — keeping the motivation loop intact without you having to think about it.
This is what separates session 1 from session 30. The first time you use it, it helps you start. By session 30, it works with your brain to get ahead of the blocks before they fully set in.
